FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions to consider before we start.

How much does a website cost?

Current packages start at €350, €650 and €950. The final quote depends on pages, functionality and available content.

How long does a project take?

Essential: 5–7 business days. Professional: 10–15 business days. Tailored: from 15 business days depending on scope. The timeline starts once I receive the materials and confirm the content. Revisions and your response times can affect delivery.

Do I need to provide the text?

For every package, you provide your business and service information, your logo if available, and photographs you have permission to use. I organise the content and make basic clarity edits. If photographs are missing, I can select appropriately licensed free images. Full copywriting from scratch, translations and paid images are quoted separately.

Are domain and hosting included?

You register and renew the domain in your name; I help connect it. Hosting depends on your website's needs and any provider fee is paid directly by you. Initial publication and agreed domain and hosting setup are included in all three packages. Optional maintenance and post-delivery content changes are quoted separately.

What happens after launch?

I check the form, links and display before handover. Initial support covers 14 calendar days for Essential and Professional, or 30 calendar days for Tailored, to report and fix display, link or form issues relating to the delivered work. New text, images, pages and features are quoted separately.

How many revisions are included?

Essential and Professional include 2 rounds; Tailored includes 3. Each round groups your feedback into one list for adjustments within the agreed scope. New pages or features are quoted separately.

Do you guarantee sales or Google rankings?

No. I do not promise guaranteed sales or Google rankings.
