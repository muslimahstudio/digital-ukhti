LESSON 02 · PLANNING

What you need before creating your website.

A good website does not start with writing code. It starts by defining what you want to achieve, whom you want to reach and what information that person needs to find.

Level · Beginner Time · 15–20 min Path · Web design

01

Start with your goal.

Before choosing colours, images or fonts, you need to answer one question:

What is the purpose of this website?

A website can have different goals: introducing a business, attracting clients, showcasing a portfolio, selling products, taking bookings, sharing content or gaining subscribers.

Without a clear goal, it will be difficult to decide what content you need and what action visitors should take.

02

Define your target audience.

You do not need to know every detail about your ideal customer, but you should have a clear idea of who will use your website.

01

Who is that person?

02

What problem or need do they have?

03

What information are they looking for?

04

What should they do after visiting your page?

03

Decide which pages you need.

A small website does not need twenty pages. It is better to start with a clear structure.

01

Home

Briefly explains what you do and who you help.

02

About

Introduces the project, brand or business.

03

Services

Explains what you offer and how you can help.

04

Portfolio

Shows projects, examples or completed work.

05

Contact

Makes it easy for someone to contact you.

06

Resources

Adds useful content if it is part of your strategy.

04

Prepare your content before designing.

One common mistake is designing a page before knowing what text it will contain.

Before opening your editor, prepare at least:

01

Your website's main heading.

02

A short description of your project.

03

Descriptions of your services or products.

04

Calls to action.

05

Contact information.

05

Define a basic visual identity.

You do not need a complex brand identity to get started, but you do need visual consistency.

COLOURS

A simple palette.

Choose two to four main colours to use throughout your website.

FONTS

Keep them few and readable.

Use one or two font families to maintain consistency.

Visual consistency is often more important than using lots of decorative elements.

06

Prepare your images.

Images can make a website look professional or completely disorganised.

Try to use images with a similar style and avoid uploading excessively large files.

It is also helpful to give files descriptive names, for example:

web-design-digital-ukhti.jpg

07

Think about your domain.

If your project will have a professional online presence, it is worth considering your domain name from the beginning.

A good domain is usually:

01

Easy to remember.

02

Easy to type.

03

Related to your brand.

04

As short as possible.

SUMMARY

Before starting your website, define:

  • Your main goal.
  • Your target audience.
  • The pages you need.
  • Your main copy.
  • Your call to action.
  • Your colours and fonts.
  • Your images.
  • Your domain.

PUT IT INTO PRACTICE

Plan your first website.

Before moving to the next lesson, open a document or take a sheet of paper and answer:

  1. What is my website's goal?
  2. Who do I want to visit it?
  3. Which pages do I need?
  4. What will my main call to action be?
  5. What content have I prepared?
  6. What content am I still missing?

Save your answers. You will use them later when we start building the page.
