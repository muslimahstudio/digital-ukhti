Digital Ukhti

A clear, professional digital presence for a growing digital studio.

The Project

A website built around clarity

Digital Ukhti presents web design and development, digital presence analysis, SEO, and market and competitor research services. Its website helps visitors understand each service, explore projects and request a quote.

Visual Direction

A refined and recognisable identity

Black, white, ivory and beige create a restrained editorial design. Dark gold highlights key buttons and links. Generous spacing and clear hierarchy keep content accessible.

Identity

Brand, palette and typography

The brand identity is designed for digital use. Warm neutrals and dark gold provide consistency; Cormorant Garamond is used for headings and Inter for body text and navigation.

Web Design

Services presented simply

The homepage introduces the studio, highlights projects and provides two calls to action. Service pages lead to the contact form with the selected service prefilled; a floating mobile button helps visitors make contact.

Digital Experience

Consistent across screens

Clear navigation, responsive layouts and a coherent visual system across desktop, tablet and mobile.

Development

HTML, CSS and responsive design

A semantic HTML structure, custom CSS, JavaScript and WebP imagery support the site and its continued development.

Result

A foundation for future growth

The result is Digital Ukhti's own services website, with portfolio, resources and a clear route to contact.
