THE PROJECT

Building a clear, professional digital presence.

Digital Ukhti offers web design and development, digital presence analysis, SEO, and market and competitor research. Its website serves entrepreneurs, professionals and small businesses.

The goal is to explain each service clearly, showcase projects and make it easy for prospective clients to request a quote.

VISUAL DIRECTION

A restrained, modern and recognisable identity.

The visual direction combines black, white, ivory and beige. Deep gold highlights buttons and links within a restrained layout with generous spacing and a clear visual hierarchy.

IDENTITY

Brand

Development of the Digital Ukhti visual concept and an identity designed for digital media.

Colour palette

Warm neutrals form the base, with deep gold on buttons and links to maintain contrast and consistency across pages.

Typography

Cormorant Garamond for headings and Inter for body text, navigation and buttons combine editorial character with readability.

WEB DESIGN

Designed to make services easy to understand.

The homepage combines a main message, two calls to action and a portfolio preview. Backgrounds and layouts alternate to present projects, services and the working process.

Service pages lead to the contact form with the relevant service preselected. On mobile, a fixed button keeps contact within reach while scrolling.
