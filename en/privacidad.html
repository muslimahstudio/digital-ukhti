Privacy policy

Information about the data you share when contacting Digital Ukhti and how we measure visits to the website.

Contact

For questions about how we use your personal data, email holadigitalukhti@gmail.com. The data controller’s legal identity will be added once the business is formally registered.

Data you provide

When you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, project type if you select one, and the message you write. We need your name, email address and message to respond. The project type is optional.

Purpose and legal basis

We use this data to respond to your enquiry and, if you request a quote, to assess your project and prepare the quote. Processing a quote request is based on taking steps at your request before potentially entering into a contract. Submitting the form does not subscribe you to a marketing list.

Service providers

The form is submitted through Formspree. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers may process data needed to deliver their services. We will review their terms and any potential international data transfers before publishing the final version of this policy.

Data retention

To be confirmed: the retention period for enquiries and quotes, and how it will be applied in email and Formspree. No retention period has been configured yet.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, ask for processing to be restricted, or exercise other applicable rights by emailing holadigitalukhti@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

Analytics and cookies

If you accept analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) to understand which pages people visit and how they reach the website. The Google tag loads only after you accept. Google Analytics may use cookies such as _ga to distinguish browsers and process usage and device data. The legal basis is your consent. You can decline analytics and still use the website.

To change or withdraw your choice, select the “Privacy” button on Digital Ukhti pages. Once you withdraw consent, the website stops sending new visits to Analytics and deletes the Analytics cookies it can identify on this domain. You can also delete cookies in your browser. Read Google’s privacy policy for more information about its processing and potential international data transfers.

Under the current settings for this Google Analytics property, event data is retained for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months. The option to reset the user-data retention period with each new activity is enabled, so the period may start again if you revisit the website. These settings do not by themselves determine how long cookies last or how long aggregated analytics reports are retained.
