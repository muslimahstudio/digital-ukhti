NURÉA

A botanical identity and e-commerce experience designed to make skincare feel like a mindful ritual.

The Project

Botanical beauty with a serene identity

NURÉA is a fictional botanical cosmetics brand. The project covers visual strategy, brand identity, a six-product collection, packaging and desktop/mobile e-commerce concepts.

Challenge & Strategy

Calm, clear and approachable

The design balances a distinctive identity with product clarity, coherent packaging and a simple, responsive shopping journey.

Visual Direction

Calm, texture and nature

A restrained palette, natural textures, soft light and editorial typography shape the visual system.

Identity

A recognisable visual language

The logo and N monogram support consistency across labels, packaging and digital formats.

Collection

Six formulas, one experience

Renewing Botanical Serum

Balancing Face Cream

Radiance Facial Oil

Gentle Facial Cleanser

Purifying Mineral Mask

Restorative Body Balm

Packaging

Coherent at every touchpoint

Digital Experience

An informed shopping journey

Homepage, product page and basket/checkout mockups prioritise imagery, essential product information and clear navigation.

Brand Applications

Recognisable digital communication

Result

A responsive design proposal

The outcome is a collection of brand, packaging and e-commerce mockups for a fictional business. It is not a published shop and no commercial performance is claimed.
