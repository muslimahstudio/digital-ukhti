CONCEPT PROJECT · STUDIO INITIATIVE

NURÉA: from concept to a clear shopping journey.

An original project that explores Digital Ukhti’s design approach. NURÉA is a fictional brand, and the images and screens are design proposals.

Design challenge

The challenge was to present a botanical skincare brand and organise its catalogue so shoppers could easily find product information.

Design decisions

A neutral palette, product photography and editorial typography create continuity. The homepage, product page and basket give information and next steps distinct places.

Design outcome

The visual system and desktop and mobile mockups show the proposed shopping journey. The project has no sales metrics or customer validation.

01 · The project

Botanical beauty with a quietly distinctive identity

NURÉA is a fictional botanical skincare brand for people seeking simple formulas, familiar ingredients and a more mindful skincare routine.

The project covers visual strategy, brand identity, the product collection, packaging and a digital shopping experience that works consistently on desktop and mobile.

The aim was to convey nature, trust and sophistication while developing a visual identity distinctive within natural skincare.

02 · Challenge and strategy

A calm, clear and approachable experience

Differentiation

Create a recognisable identity that combines botanical influences, editorial elegance and a contemporary aesthetic.

Consistency

Develop a flexible visual system for products, packaging, communications and digital platforms.

Conversion

Design a simple, responsive shopping journey centred on essential product information.

03 · Visual direction

Calm, texture and nature

Moodboard Textures · Natural light · Botanicals

04 · Brand identity

A delicate, recognisable visual system

The logo pairs high-contrast editorial typography with an organic gold line. The N monogram identifies the brand at small sizes, on labels, seals and digital elements.

05 · Collection

Six formulas, one consistent experience

01 · Facial treatment

Renewing Botanical Serum

02 · Hydration

03 · Nourishment

04 · Cleansing

06 · Packaging

A consistent identity in every detail

07 · Digital experience

An online shop designed to guide purchase decisions

The interface prioritises product photography, essential information and simple navigation. The responsive design maintains the same hierarchy and personality across screens.

Homepage

An editorial homepage with direct access to the collection and layouts for desktop and mobile.

Product page

Benefits, ingredients, ratings, pricing and purchase options are arranged in a clear structure.

Basket and checkout

A checkout concept designed around a simple, focused purchase flow.

08 · Brand applications

A recognisable digital presence

09 · Result

A visual system and a responsive shopping concept.

The project brings together brand identity, packaging and e-commerce screens for a fictional brand. The mockups show how visual hierarchy and a consistent identity carry through to mobile. The shop is a concept and has no measured commercial results.
