CONCEPT PROJECT · BRAND IDENTITY · WEB DESIGN

La Prun

A warm, natural and welcoming identity for a fictional café and local produce shop.

The Project

Building a clear digital presence

La Prun is a conceptual café and local produce shop combining tradition, community and a contemporary experience. The project explores a recognisable identity and a clear digital presence.

Visual Direction

Warm, natural and welcoming

Inspired by nature, artisanal products and local life, the visual direction uses warm tones and understated elements to convey authenticity and quality.

Identity

A consistent visual system

The brand concept uses charcoal, cream, clay and olive tones, with Cormorant Garamond for headings and Inter for navigation and body copy.

CHARCOALCREAMCLAYOLIVE

Web Design

Simple discovery of products and experiences

The website concept offers straightforward navigation, a responsive layout and clear sections introducing the café and its local products.

Digital Experience

Clarity on every screen

Navigation, typography, spacing and visual hierarchy are designed to remain consistent on desktop, tablet and mobile.

Development

Structure, design and adaptation

The demonstration uses HTML and CSS with a responsive approach and a structure that can accommodate future content.

Result

A flexible conceptual website

A cohesive identity and website concept designed as a portfolio demonstration, not a commissioned client project.
